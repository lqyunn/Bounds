# Bounds
You are trapped by Bounds. It bounds you back if you run. Grab the keys that appear and find your way out. Good luck.

Move to the wall to activate next bound. Use WASD to move around. Press Q to turn on/off the flashlight.  And collect the keys. The keys will appear one at a time. Find your way out. 

Play on browser: https://lqyunn.itch.io/bounds-back
